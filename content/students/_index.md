        ## Prospective PhD students

        If you are interested in the [OIT PhD program](https://www.gsb.stanford.edu/programs/phd/fields/operations-information-technology),
        please review the program description and preparation guidance. Admissions
        decisions are made jointly by the OIT faculty, so no individual faculty member
        can make or promise an admission decision independently. If your interests align
        with the program, I strongly encourage you to [apply through the official Stanford
        GSB process](https://www.gsb.stanford.edu/programs/phd/admission).
