abstract: "Multistage decisions under uncertainty are usually attacked with simple policy classes, because the exact problem is intractable. We introduce a hierarchy of polynomial disturbance-feedback policies, each computable from a single semidefinite program and indexed by the polynomial’s degree. Raising the degree buys accuracy at a predictable computational price."
